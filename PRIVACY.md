# Prediction Cup: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

Prediction Cup ("the app") is a predictions app that runs inside Reddit communities on Reddit's Developer Platform. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

## What we store

When you make a call in a community that uses Prediction Cup, the app stores, **inside that community's app storage on Reddit's platform**:

- your Reddit user ID and username, plus your Reddit avatar image link, to show you on leaderboards;
- the options you picked on each prediction;
- your points, number of right calls, number of calls, and streak;
- whether you turned on results alerts, and how many alerts you were sent that day (to enforce the daily limit).

The app also notes who created each prediction (a moderator, or a member adding one to a hub), and keeps a list of the predictions you created and called so it can show you your **My activity** tab. Only you can see that tab.

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any information outside Reddit. The app makes no requests to services outside Reddit.

## How we use it

Only to run the app: recording calls, scoring them, showing leaderboards and your stats, giving rank flair if the community's moderators turned that on, and sending results alerts if you turned them on. We do not sell, rent, or share your data, use it for advertising, profile you, or use it to train AI models.

## What is public

Leaderboards show usernames, avatars, points and accuracy to members of the community. The split of picks on each prediction is shown as totals, not as who picked what. If you press **Comment my call**, the comment is posted publicly from your account, like any other comment. If you post a prediction, it is posted publicly from your account, like any other post, after you give the app permission.

## Deletion

- When a prediction post is deleted, the picks and results stored for it are deleted, and the points it gave are taken off the leaderboards.
- When a Reddit account is deleted, the app removes that user ID and the data linked to it within about 30 days.
- If moderators uninstall the app, Reddit removes the app's storage for that community.
- You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by messaging u/kayalcore on Reddit.

## Notifications

Results alerts are off unless you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time from the **You** tab. You'll get at most 2 alerts a day.

## Children

Prediction Cup is not directed to children under 13, and it follows Reddit's own age requirements.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be posted here.
